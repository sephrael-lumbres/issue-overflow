Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Issue Overflow is a personal project run by one developer and used by a small group of friends and family. This page says what it stores, what that is used for, and what you can do about it. Questions go to support@issueoverflow.app.

What it stores
  • Your account: your name, email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash, never in readable form), your role, your organization and projects, your notification settings, and whether you have verified your email address.
  • What you create: organizations, projects, issues, comments, attachments and your profile picture.
  • Change history: when an issue, project or account changes, the earlier values, who made the change and when.
  • Messages you send with the Contact Us form (your name, email address and message) are emailed to support@issueoverflow.app and kept in that mailbox, not in the app.

There are no payments, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What it is used for

Only to run the app: signing you in, showing your organization's work to its members, sending the emails below, and answering your messages. Your data isn't sold or used for anything else.

Who can see it
  • Members of your organization see your name, email address, role and profile picture. Issues, comments and attachments are visible to the members of the project they belong to.
  • The services that host the app and its database and that deliver its email store or handle data on its behalf. Attachments and profile pictures are stored with Amazon Web Services (S3). Everything is stored in the United States.
  • If the law requires it, data may be shared to answer a valid legal request.
Other services your browser contacts

Attachments and profile pictures download straight from Amazon S3 through short-lived links. Pages also load Bootstrap, Font Awesome and Feather icons from public content delivery networks (jsDelivr and cdnjs). In both cases, those services see your IP address and browser type.

Email

The app emails you to verify your address and to reset your password, when you ask for it, and sends notifications about issues and comments only if you turn them on under Account > Notifications.

Cookies

There is one cookie, JSESSIONID, which keeps you signed in. It holds no personal information and ends when you log out or after a while without activity. Your browser also remembers whether you collapsed the side menu. There are no other cookies.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while your account exists. If a Project Manager removes you from your organization, the issues you created and the files you uploaded are deleted; your account stays. Deleting your account (Account > Security) deletes your account, the issues you created, your comments and the files you uploaded. If you were the last member of your organization, the organization and its projects are deleted too. The change history can keep earlier values, including names and email addresses, after the record they belong to is deleted. Backups, if any, expire on the hosting provider's schedule. To have anything that remains removed, email support@issueoverflow.app.

Changes
